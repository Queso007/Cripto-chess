How it works — read this before staking

  • Stablecoins only: USDT, USDC, RLUSD. Nothing else is accepted. A $20 stake is exactly 20 USDT, 20 USDC, or 20 RLUSD — amounts never change value between deposit and payout, and there is no price conversion.
  • Win: you keep your stake plus 90% of the loser's stake. The operator keeps 10% of the loser's stake.
  • Draw: each player is refunded 95%; the operator keeps 5% each.
  • Game never starts: deposits are refunded in full.
  • A playing game ends only on checkmate, resignation, draw, or the chess-clock flag. Disconnects do not end a game in progress.
  • Custodial escrow: stakes are held in the operator's escrow wallet during play. You trust the operator — this is not trustless on-ledger escrow.
  • Matching deposits: on XRPL, deposits are matched by destination tag (the proven xApp pattern). On Tron, Solana, Polygon, and Ethereum there are no destination tags, so deposits are matched by the sender address you register when you create the game, plus the exact token and exact amount.
  • Real-money gaming: you can lose your stake. You must be legally allowed to play for stakes in your jurisdiction. Pennsylvania real-money gaming compliance is an open, unresolved question for this app — this is not legal advice.
